5案の比較用。選択後にICOへ変換し、exeへ埋め込む。

Combine a clear readable document silhouette with a three-node diagram, as one unified application mark. A pale cyan folded corner and rich blue body, white diagram, extremely clean geometric construction. No text lines cluttering the symbol.
